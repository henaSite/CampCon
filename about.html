About Campus Connect

Campus Connect (CampCon) is a professional, secure full-stack platform designed to help students and faculty quickly report, discover, and recover lost items across campus.

How It Works

  • Report: Submit a detailed report for any item you lost or found on university grounds.
  • Moderate: Campus moderators review submissions to ensure safety and prevent spam.
  • Discover: Browse the live community feed filtered by category, search keywords, or item type.
  • Connect: Leave public lead comments or start secure private conversations with item owners.

Privacy & Safety

CampCon protects your personal contact details. Other users only see your name and profile when you post items or send messages. Role-based moderation (Super Admin, Admin, Moderator) ensures campus-wide trust and accountability.
